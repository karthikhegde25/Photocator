Every photo, stamped with where it happened.

Photocator is a free, open-source Android camera that adds the place, the map, the weather and the time to every photo and video you take.

Features

Proof of place, built into the shutter.

Take a photo and the geotag is part of it: precise, readable and yours to arrange.

📍

Live geotag

Coordinates, Plus Code, altitude, speed, place name and full address, drawn right on the viewfinder before you shoot.

🗺️

Map thumbnail

A map of the exact spot in 11 styles, from satellite and streets to topographic and dark gray.

⛅

Weather & compass

Temperature, conditions, humidity, wind, magnetic field and compass heading, switchable with one toggle.

🎬

Geotagged video

Record video with the geotag burned into every frame, rotated to match how you hold the phone.

🖼️

Add to any photo

Pick a PNG or JPG from your gallery, choose a location on the map and stamp it after the fact.

✏️

Edit before saving

Review every shot, change the address, add a note, move the geotag or put it in a strip below the photo.

🏷️

Your watermark

Upload your own logos and show one in the corner of the geotag, globally or for a single photo.

📐

Size & position

Drag and resize the geotag exactly where you want it, and save it as your default.

🎛️

Camera controls

Zoom dial, flash, timer, grid, aspect ratio, image quality and volume-button shutter or zoom.

Modes

Photo. Night. Video.

Three modes, one tap apart, all with the same geotag.

Photo

Sharp everyday shots with the geotag baked in.

Night

Uses your phone's night extension, or a low-light boost where it isn't available.

Video

Record with sound and a live, rotating geotag.

How it works

Point. Shoot. Done.

Allow camera, photos and location

Photocator asks once, in order, and explains what each permission is for.

Wait for the GPS lock

The geotag appears over the viewfinder as soon as a position is found. GPS is preferred, with network location as backup.

Shoot, review, save

Check the photo, tweak the geotag if you like, and save. Files go to Pictures/Photocator and Movies/Photocator on your phone.

Privacy policy

Your photos stay on your phone.

Last updated: October 2026

What Photocator does not do

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no ads and no analytics or tracking tools.
  • The developer does not receive, collect or store your photos, videos, location or any personal data.
  • Nothing you shoot is uploaded to any server run by the developer.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • Camera to take photos and videos.
  • Microphone to record sound with video. Asked only when you use Video mode.
  • Location to read your GPS position for the geotag.
  • Photos and media to save your files and show the last photo and your videos.
  • Internet for the lookups listed below.

Third-party services

To build the geotag, the app sends your coordinates (or a place you search for) to these public services. They have their own privacy policies:

  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim and Photon turn coordinates into a place name and address, and power place search.
  • Open-Meteo provides the weather for your coordinates.
  • Esri and OpenStreetMap tile servers provide the map images. Tile requests reveal the map area you are viewing.

Your phone's IP address is visible to these services, as with any internet request. The app does not send your photos to them.

Data on your device

Settings, your watermark images and cached map tiles are stored locally in the app's private storage. Photos and videos are saved in your gallery folders and can be deleted at any time. Uninstalling the app removes its private data.

Sharing

Your geotagged photos contain your location. Think before posting them publicly, since anyone who sees them can see where they were taken.

Children

Photocator does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page. For questions, open an issue at github.com/karthikhegde25/Photocator/issues.
